# House of the Dragon — The Ashen Crown (Rhaenyra)

A hardcore political-realicard campaign for [WorldArchitect.AI](https://worldarchitect.ai). **You play Queen Rhaenyra Targaryen** in the immediate aftermath of the [House of the Dragon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_of_the_Dragon_(TV_series)) Season 3 finale (post-Tumbleton, post-Helaena-suicide, post-Gullet). Jace has been dead for ~60 days (died S3 E1 at the Battle of the Gullet). Aegon lives. The treasury is empty. The smallfolk are at the gates. **Quad-Pillar mechanics** track your reign in real-time. **No canonical endings** — what kind of queen survives is your choice.

This is a companion to [House of the Dragon — The Bastard's Claim](house-of-the-dragon-campaign.md) (a gender-ambiguous Daemon-bastard on the eve of the Dance). The Ashen Crown starts *years later*, at the war's worst moment, playing Rhaenyra canon.

> **Hardcore mode.** Every choice has a primary gain, an immediate cost, and a delayed consequence. The AI Dungeon Master will not let you "win" a scene by stacking modifiers; the cost is paid somewhere, even if not in the same turn. There are no perfect victories.

**Show anchor:** Anchored to the *House of the Dragon* TV canon (post-Season-3 finale). **Book fallback:** Where TV cut off or diverged from GRRM's [*Fire & Blood*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fire_%26_Blood) (2018), the bible notes `[Book fallback]` for the AI DM to consider. **Canon-correction:** Corlys Velaryon is Jace's **grandfather** (Jace was Rhaenyra + Laenor Velaryon's son); Corlys cannot claim Jace as "my son." Addam of Hull is a **loyal dragonseed** legitimized as a Velaryon in S3 E3, NOT a traitor.

You are Queen Rhaenyra Targaryen. The throne is yours. Your son is dead. Your rival lives. Your court despises you. Your dragon waits.

Universal 20-Level Progression anchor stats: L11 Huge Syrax Start (295 HP AC 18, 16d6/60ft, 280 gp/day); L12 Gargantuan Balerion (350 HP AC 19, 18d6/60ft, 400 gp/day); L16 Colossal (620 HP AC 21, 26d6/90ft, 1000 gp/day); L20 Colossal Apex 2× Balerion (1050 HP AC 24, 36d6/120ft, 2000 gp/day). Control DC = breath DC; rider must beat it on >20% HP dmg/round.

Dual-HP Combat (Rider + Dragon independent pools; >40 dmg in 1 turn = DC 15 STR/DEX to stay mounted; Scorpion Bolts = 4d10+10 + DC 16 CON vs flight loss on crit eye/wing).

Rider Bond Toggle (player choice each turn): **Bonded** (+5 Control DC saved) vs **Unleashed** (+10 dmg, no commands).

Collateral Damage: fire in populated areas = 100×Tier gp/round, -5% CS/round, -25% regional tax.

**Level-Up XP Rule (per user 2026-08-13):** XP = 0.34 × (next_level_threshold − current_level_threshold) per die roll (attack/save/ability check/skill check). Standard D&D 5e cumulative thresholds: L2=300, L3=900, L4=2700, L5=6500, L6=14000, L8=34000, L10=64000, L11=85000, L14=140000, L16=195000, L20=355000 (interpolate from 5e table). Worked: at L2 (300 XP) aiming for L3 (900) = 0.34 × 600 = 204 XP per die roll. 3 rolls = 612 XP → L3. **NO XP during god mode or time-frozen turns** (level-up modal, character creation, downtime bookkeeping, fast-travel, narrator-driven cutscenes). Bonus damage rolls do not separately award XP — only the to-hit/save/ability-check roll.

Ascension Reputation Tiers (auto at level): L15 Recognized Sovereign (Royal Command DC +2, CHA→22, PTR floor 50); L17 Rightful Queen (1×/Reaction advantage ally in 60 ft; +5 PTR if mercy shown); L19 Apex Queen (Syrax auto-succeeds Control DC; cannot flee losing battle).

## More Details

### Customize the character

The default setup plays **Queen Rhaenyra canon at age 32 post-Tumbleton**. You can override anything in the **Character you want to play** field — e.g.:

- **Different era** — set the bible to play *before* Tumbleton (S2 finale or earlier) by editing the timeline in the bible's SECTION 1 anchor text
- **Different Quad-Pillar baseline** — adjust the four CS/TL/PTR/DL starting values in SECTION 7 if you want a less-crisis or more-crisis start
- **Different dragon** — swap Syrax for a different mount (caraxes, vermithor, seasmoke) by changing the L11 mount in SECTION 4
- **Different class** — the Bard chassis is fixed, but you can drop the Dragonrider subclass entirely and just play a Bard L11+ queen if you want a less-mechanical run
### Use a different HotD bible** — if you want to play the *Bastard's Claim* (Daemon's gender-ambiguous bastard on the eve of the Dance) instead, see [House of the Dragon — The Bastard's Claim](house-of-the-dragon-campaign.md)

The bible is robust to most player-shaped edits. The wizard accepts the full ~16,000-character slimmed version above without truncation.

### L25 Divine Ascension — what to know

The Divine Ascension track at L25 is **optional and gated.** Most campaigns will not reach it. If you do reach it (campaign must run L11 → L25, roughly 14 Acts), pick a single Domain and the AI DM unlocks the right-of-petition mechanic, immunity to disease, and CHA cap removal. Syrax becomes your divine portfolio — if she dies, you take 1 level of permanent Exhaustion. This is the campaign's ultimate reward for sustained play.

### XP rule & god-mode boundary

The XP rule (`0.34 × Δthreshold` per die roll) is calibrated for sustained play. **God mode skips do not award XP** — narrator-driven cutscenes, time-skips, scripted sequences, and modal screens (level-up, character creation, downtime bookkeeping) all freeze XP. The DM must award XP per Section 8 of the bible, not as bonuses to other rolls. If the DM forgets, ask for the per-die-roll award.
