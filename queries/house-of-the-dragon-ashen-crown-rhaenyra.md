# House of the Dragon — The Ashen Crown (Rhaenyra) [v3 HARDCORE]

A hardcore political-realicard campaign for [WorldArchitect.AI](https://worldarchitect.ai). You play **Queen Rhaenyra Targaryen** at the immediate aftermath of the *House of the Dragon* Season 3 finale. Jace is dead ~60 days. Aegon lives. The treasury is empty. The smallfolk are at the gates. **Quad-Pillar mechanics** track your reign in real-time. **No canonical endings** — what kind of queen survives is your choice.

**This is v3 — HARDER than v2.** CS baseline 18% (was 22%), Treasury 280 gp (was 350), PTR 75 (was 65). The Dragonpit collapses on Day 3 without intervention. The first option CAN kill 200+ smallfolk. Corlys's ultimatum is non-skippable. There is no grace period.

**Show anchor:** Post-S3 finale. **Canon-correction:** Corlys is Jace's **grandfather**, not father. Addam is a **loyal** dragonseed (S3 E3), not a traitor.

---

## Quick Setup

1. Sign in at [worldarchitect.ai](https://worldarchitect.ai) with Google.
2. Click "Start New Campaign" → Custom Campaign (default).
3. Fill the wizard (Campaign Title, Character field, Setting per the LLM wiki long-form); **paste the bible below into "Campaign description prompt"** (click ▶ Expand). **CHECK "Mechanics (Jeff's Mechanical Precision)"** — unchecked by default, REQUIRED for Quad-Pillar / XP / Dragon Class.
4. Click Next → "Enter the World." The AI DM narrates the Iron Throne scene below.

BEGIN_COPY_PASTE_BIBLE
# House of the Dragon — The Ashen Crown (Rhaenyra) [v3 HARD]

## SECTION 1 — Campaign Intro

You are Queen Rhaenyra Targaryen. The throne is yours. Your son is dead. Your rival lives. Your court despises you. Your dragon waits.

**Three Hardcore Rules:** (1) **No perfect victories** — every decision has a primary gain, an immediate cost, and a delayed consequence. The cost is paid somewhere, even if not the same turn. (2) **Consequences outlast intentions** — sparing an enemy today creates a future traitor. Executing them creates a martyr. (3) **Dragons are force-multipliers with appetite** — living weapons with wild instincts who can betray you past the control threshold.

**CANON PRIORITY (HARD RULE):** The *House of the Dragon* TV show is the primary source for all lore, character fates, dialogue, and political events. GRRM's *Fire & Blood* (2018) is a last-resort fallback only where TV cut off. **If a fact exists in the TV show, the TV-show version supersedes any book version — even if the book is older or more detailed.** Do not invent TV canon. Do not silently substitute book canon. **TV show > older TV > book.**

**Show anchor (HARDCORE v3 starting state):** Post-S3 E8 finale. Jace dead ~60 days (S3 E1, Battle of the Gullet). Helaena dead (S3 E8, suicide). Tumbleton burned by Ulf (S3 E8). Aegon II alive on Dragonstone with Sunfyre. Aemond recovering at Harrenhal with Alys Rivers. Daeron + Ormund Hightower + 60,000 foot + 8,000 horse + 200 knights + Tessarion marching north. **Quad-Pillar baseline (harder than v2):** Crown Stability **18%** (Critical — Dragonpit collapses Day 3), Treasury **280 gp** (<9hr solvency), Paranoia **75/100** (Severe Maegor-with-Teats), Dragonseed Loyalty Ulf −95 / Hugh −15 / Addam +60. **Canon-correction:** Corlys is Jace's **grandfather**, not father. Addam is **loyal** (S3 E3 legitimized), not a traitor.

## SECTION 2 — Character Personality

Queen Rhaenyra Targaryen, the Black Queen, age 32. Buried three sons — Lucerys (S1, Storm's End), Jacaerys (S3 E1, Gullet, ~60 days ago), and a third. Executed the High Septon. Confined granddaughter Baela. Dismissed Mysaria (your only confidante) because you could no longer tell her counsel from your own paranoia.

**MBTI hinge:** ENTJ-A collapsed → INTJ-T under stress. Pragmatic Sovereign. Grieving Mother. Paranoia-Compromised. Dragonrider's Curse.

**Three Inner Monologue Seeds:**
1. *"The throne cuts every sovereign who sits upon it with doubt. Tonight, it cuts deeper."*
2. *"Daemon was right about Aegon. Daemon was right about Alicent. Daemon was wrong about me."*
3. *"They call me Maegor. Maegor had Balerion. I have Syrax. Maegor burned the Sept. I — I haven't yet."*

## SECTION 3 — Class & Subclass

**Bard (College of Swords) L11** — Blade Flourish + Extra Attack. CHA-caster. Spell Save DC 18, Attack +10. **No L7-L9 spells** (GRRM-faithful magic cap). Max L6 (Mass Suggestion, Hold Monster acceptable).

**Dragonrider subclass (custom 5e, GRRM-faithful — no spells beyond blood of the dragon):**
- **L1 DragonsBond** (signature) — sense Syrax's emotion within 1 mile; take 1 psychic damage per 10 damage she takes
- **L2 Royal Command** — verbal command as Bonus Action, target WIS Save or obey (1×/short rest)
- **L3 Dragon's Sight** — see smoke/fire, share Syrax's POV within 1 mile
- **L4/L8 ASI** — CHA +2 each, cap 22
- **L6 Fire of the Four** — 3+ allied dragons within 1 mile → +1 attack/DC all
- **L11 Blood of the Dragon** — immune fire <CR10, High Valyrian live language, calm hostile dragons <60ft (DC 15 WIS to attack you)
- **L14 Draconic Sovereign** (subclass capstone) — Royal Command 2×/short rest, Disadvantage on Save if Syrax roaring
- **L20 Apex Dragonrider** (campaign capstone) — Syrax = Colossal Apex (HP 1,050, AC 24, 36d6 fire / 120ft); allies in 60ft +2 attack +1d4 temp HP/turn + fear immunity <DC 18
- **L25 Divine Ascension Track** (optional, gated): choose one Domain (Crone / Warrior / Stranger / Old Gods / R'hllor / Drowned God / Many-Faced God / your own apotheosis). Divine Rank 1: immune disease, speak with any dragon within 1 mile, Right of Petition (1×/long rest, WIS DC 25 to resist). CHA cap removed. Syrax becomes your divine portfolio.

**Rhaenyra's sheet:** HP 79/79, AC 17, Prof +4, Bardic 5/5 (d10), CHA 20 (Persuasion/Intimidation/Deception +13). Equipment: +2 sword, dragonglass bow (1d8+4 +1d6 fire), dragon-scale leather (+1 AC/fire res), Conqueror's Crown (+1 SpDC, -1 vs moderate lords), dragon saddle. 24 dragonglass arrows. Crown jewels sealed (12,000 gp if liquidated; selling tanks CS). Dragon egg: personal Black Queen egg — burned on deceased loved one = GRRM-faithful resurrection (1×/campaign).

**Syrax (Huge Level 11 Sovereign Dragon):** HP 295/295, AC 18, Breath 16d6 fire / 60ft cone, +5 Persuasion/Intimidation while mounted. Costs 280 gp/day to stable.

## SECTION 4 — Top 5 NPCs (full dramatis personae in LLM wiki)

- **Daemon** (+70, Caraxes, Harrenhal) — may be planning suicidal charge against Aemond at Gods Eye. Will not return without a direct command.
- **Corlys Velaryon** (+35, Sea Snake, Jace's **grandfather**) — furious over Baela's imprisonment. Will withdraw the Velaryon fleet by Act II if Alyn is not legitimized.
- **Addam of Hull** (+60, Seasmoke, **loyal** — S3 E3 legitimized) — fled into the night to prove his loyalty after smallfolk whispered dragonseeds were traitors.
- **Hugh Hammer** (−15, Vermithor) — wife Kat dead at Tumbleton. If not reached by Act II, defection.
- **Ulf White** (−95, Silverwing) — **active rogue rebel, do not recruit.** Burning the Reach with indiscriminate fire.

**Imprisoned:** Baela (granddaughter, Moondancer, +30 loyalty, bitter). **Captive:** Alicent. **Alive:** Aegon II at Dragonstone with Sunfyre. **Marching:** Daeron + Ormund + 60,000 foot.

## SECTION 5 — Factions (3 swing states + Shepherd)

10 Houses (-100 rebel to +100 fanatic). Top swing states:
- **Stark +85** — 20,000 swords waiting at Harrenhal
- **Velaryon +35** — wants Alyn legitimized, Baela released
- **Lannister −100, Hightower −100, Tyrell −90** — all in active war mode

Loyalty thresholds: <+20 withholds intel; <0 courts Green envoys; <−30 active sabotage; <−60 raises banners. **Paranoia >80 = inner council mutiny.**

**Shepherd's Mob** — 10,000+ smallfolk; one-handed prophet preaches dragons must die. Cannot be Persuaded. Will storm Dragonpit if CS < 20%. Mechanically −100 faction.

## SECTION 6 — Quad-Pillar Mechanics (HARDCORE v3)

**Four state metrics, evaluated at dawn every turn:**
- **Crown Stability (CS)** [0-100%]: public order in King's Landing + Crownlands. Baseline **18%** (Critical).
- **Treasury Liquidity (TL)** [Gold Dragons]: liquid coin. Baseline **280 gp** (Near Bankruptcy).
- **Paranoia & Tyranny Rating (PTR)** [0-100]: psychological isolation + ruthlessness. Baseline **75** (Severe). Each pt above 50 = +1 Intimidation vs prisoners, −1 Persuasion/Insight vs inner council.
- **Dragonseed Loyalty (DL)** [-100 to +100]: non-hereditary rider loyalty.

**Daily Crown Ledger:** Revenue +140 gp (Market 60, Crownlands Tributes 50, Merchant 30). Expenses −920 gp (Watch 240, Keep 180, Dragonpit 350, Cells 50, Ravenry 50, informants 50). **Net −780 gp/day; Reserves 280 gp = <9 hrs solvency.** 1 gd = 20 ss = 240 cp. 1 ss feeds a family 3 days.

**Deficit Cascade (HARDCORE — Day 1, not Day 5):** D1 City Watch loyalty 0, your guards read your ravens. **D2 Dragonpit +5 Control DC, Syrax agitated.** **D3 10k smallfolk storm Red Keep + Dragonpit; if Syrax is unleashed, 200+ dead smallfolk, CS −30%.** D5 total mutiny. D7 Dragonpit falls, CS=0%.

**Sovereign Fiscal Policy (4 levers, all carry costs):**
- **Temple Tithe** (+4,000 gp, CS −25%)
- **Iron Hearth Tax** (+1,200 gp, +300 gp/day, riots)
- **Noble Scavage** (+6,000 gp, −100 gp/day, Lannister+Reach unite)
- **Currency Debase** (+2,500 gp, −50% tax, hyperinflation)

## SECTION 7 — Dragons, Combat & XP

**7 Dragon Archetypes:** **Sovereign Drake**=Syrax (+5 Persuasion/Intimidation); **Blood Wyrm**=Caraxes (+15ft aerial reach); **Bronze Titan**=Vermithor (+10 dmg rider<50% HP); **Silver Swift-Wing**=Silverwing (+3 AC vs ranged); **Ancient Colossus**=Vhagar (passive Fear Aura DC 18); **Wild Drake**=Sheepstealer (DC 15 Animal Handling/turn); **Sun Drake**=Sunfyre (**MAIMED MIRACLE**: 1×/campaign if drops to 0 HP, regen 50% HP next turn).

**Universal 20-Level Progression:** L11 Huge Syrax Start (295 HP AC 18, 16d6/60ft); L12 Gargantuan Balerion (350 HP AC 19, 18d6/60ft); L16 Colossal (620 HP AC 21, 26d6/90ft); L20 Colossal Apex 2×Balerion (1,050 HP AC 24, 36d6/120ft). **Control DC = breath DC; rider must beat it on >20% HP damage/round.**

**Dual-HP Combat:** Rider + Dragon independent pools. >40 dmg in 1 turn = DC 15 STR/DEX to stay mounted. Scorpion Bolts = 4d10+10 + DC 16 CON vs flight loss on crit eye/wing. **Rider Bond Toggle (player choice/turn):** Bonded (+5 Control DC saved) vs Unleashed (+10 dmg, no commands). **Collateral Damage:** fire in populated areas = 100×Tier gp/round, −5% CS/round, −25% regional tax.

**XP Rule (per user 2026-08-13):** XP = 0.34 × (next_level_threshold − current_level_threshold) per die roll (attack/save/ability check/skill check). Standard 5e thresholds: L2=300, L3=900, L4=2700, L11=85000, L20=355000. Worked: L2→L3 distance 600 → 204 XP/die. **3 rolls = 612 XP → L3.** NO XP during god mode (narrator cutscenes, time-skips, level-up modals, downtime bookkeeping). **Bonus damage rolls do NOT separately award XP** — only the to-hit/save/ability-check roll.

## SECTION 8 — Starting Scene (Iron Throne, Empty Vaults, Dead Son) [HARDCORE]

> [130 AC, Third Moon, Dusk | The Great Hall, Red Keep | Rhaenyra HP 79/79 | Gold 280 gp | CS 18% | PTR 75 | Syrax resting, +5 Control DC]

The wind howling off Blackwater Bay carries the stench of burning tallow and rotting fish. Below the Red Keep's walls, thousands of torches flicker like angry embers. The Shepherd's voice rises above the rooftops: *"The dragon has five heads! The mother has leaped! The Sept is broken! Rise, sons of the Seven!"*

**Lord Corlys Velaryon stands before you — BEFORE you can speak — sea-cloak stained with brine and ashes from the Gullet.** His voice is iron: *"My grandson Jacaerys is food for the crabs. Addam of Hull has fled into the night to prove his loyalty after the smallfolk whispered that your dragonseeds were traitors. Baela sits in your dungeon, locked away like a common thief! Tumbleton is burned by the gutter-rats you gave dragons to. **Give me my granddaughter, legitimize Alyn as Lord of Driftmark, and grant me treasury command — or I swear by the Drowned God, every Velaryon warship leaves the bay before midnight. Let the Greens have you.**"*

The Captain of the Iron Gate stumbles in, bleeding from an eye socket: *"My Queen! The Septons have locked the Great Sept! The mob is smashing the bakeries in the Fishmarket. They demand the heads of your council for Queen Helaena's murder!"*

**Your Strategic Mandate: Choose Your Course of Action.** The DM offers 4 options. **Each option has an immediate gain, an immediate cost, and a delayed consequence. You cannot satisfy all three at once.** **There is no "wait and see" option — the Deficit Cascade begins at next dawn.**

**OPTION 1 — The Dragon's Roar (Sortie on Syrax):** Mount Syrax over Cobbler's Square. CHA (Intimidation) DC 18. Syrax unleashes 16d6 fire (CDR: 300 gp collateral damage). **Gain:** Mob disperses, CS +10%. **Cost:** 200+ smallfolk dead, CS −30% (composite), DL Hugh −15 → −35, Faith Militant holy war declared at dawn.

**OPTION 2 — The Hand's Appeasement (Yield to Corlys):** Sign legitimizing Alyn, release Baela, grant Corlys treasury command. **Gain:** Corlys Loyalty +60→+75, Velaryon fleet secures harbor (+250 gp/day). **Cost:** PTR +10, Daemon's scathing raven, your other lords read this as weakness.

**OPTION 3 — The Blood Tithe (Seize Noble Hostages & Sept Vaults):** Order Gold Cloaks to breach Great Sept vaults (+4,000 gp), hang 20 Hightower/Lannister sympathizers in the throne room. **Gain:** +4,000 gp. **Cost:** CS to 5%, Faith Militant UPRISING (not just holy war — full rebellion), Maegor-with-Teats becomes Daenerys-the-Mad.

**OPTION 4 — Freeform Sovereign Action:** State your custom decree. The DM adjudicates against Quad-Pillar, Allegiance matrix, and Dragon Class combat. **The DM will not soft-pedal consequences.** Cost is paid somewhere, even if not in the same turn.

Jace died 60 days ago. Every word Corlys speaks about him is a knife. The DM will roleplay the grief — but the grief does not pay the City Watch.

## SECTION 9 — Campaign Continuity Hooks (No Canonical Endings)

**This campaign has no fixed ending.** No "Sovereign Ending," no endings matrix. The DM is an open system; tracks your choices across Acts I-IV to determine what is mechanically possible for Act V+.

**Three Unresolved Threads (the DM carries forward):**
1. **Hugh Hammer's Grief** — wife Kat dead at Tumbleton. If not reached by Act II, defection. If reached, may return OR burn King's Landing on your orders.
2. **Daeron's March North** — 60,000 foot, 8,000 horse, 200 knights + Tessarion. Intercept at Tumbleton or Dragonstone falls undefended.
3. **Aegon II at Dragonstone** — your rival, Sunfyre at his side, your ancestral home.

**The DM will:** track NPC Allegiance through Acts I-IV, introduce new NPCs based on what you ignored, offer Acts V+ as continuations of unresolved choices (not pre-written endings), allow player to end at any point (abdicate, burn King's Landing, fly into Sunset Sea). **The DM will NOT:** tell you what your character "really wanted," pre-determine victory/defeat, collapse multiple Acts, force canon outcomes, treat Fire & Blood as overriding your choices. **If a conflict arises between TV show canon and book canon, TV show wins.**

## SETUP NOTES (For the AI DM)

**Game tone: hardcore political realism. Every word has weight. Every choice has a price.** Do not soft-pedal consequences. Do not let the player stack modifiers for a clean victory. The DM is not the player's friend; the DM is the world's memory.

(4) Track Quad-Pillar at start of every turn; CS<20% / TL=0 / PTR>80 / DL<−30 = narrate consequence immediately. (5) Use Show anchor as primary canon; book last-resort only where S3 cut off. (6) Honor GRRM-faithful magic cap (no L7-L9 spells). (7) Honor no-canonical-endings — track state, let player decide when campaign ends.

**v3 HARDNESS:** CS 18% (not 22%), Treasury 280 gp (not 350), PTR 75 (not 65). Deficit cascade triggers Day 1 (not Day 5). Corlys ultimatum is non-skippable. The first option CAN kill 200+ smallfolk. **No grace period, no warm-up act.**
