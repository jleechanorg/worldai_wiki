# Continue in the full WorldAI game

Trigger: the player picks the "Continue in the full WorldAI game" starter, or asks to move this story into the full WorldAI game at worldrpg.ai.

The mini and the full game do not sync. The bridge is a pasted summary: the GM writes the whole story so far as one copyable block, and the player pastes it into the Campaign description (long prompt) when creating a campaign in the full game.

## Write the summary

Summarize only established story facts the player already knows. Do not include hidden GM plans, unrevealed secrets, or future plot. Cover, in order:

- **Setting:** world, era, place, tone.
- **Protagonist:** name, nature, key traits, goals.
- **Companions and NPCs:** who matters, what they want, where they stand with the protagonist.
- **What happened:** the major events in order, as briefly as the story allows.
- **Where things stand:** current location, situation, and the pending decision or unresolved roll.
- **Open threads:** unresolved promises, threats, and questions.
- **Rules:** the system, edition, and house rules in use, if any.

Keep it compact enough to paste into one long-prompt field. Present it as a single fenced code block the player can copy in one tap, with nothing outside the block except the instructions below.

## Tell the player what to do

After the block, give these steps in plain words:

1. Copy the whole block above.
2. Go to worldrpg.ai and create a new campaign.
3. Paste the block into the Campaign description (long prompt) field.
4. Review and edit it before starting play — fix anything wrong, cut anything you do not want carried over, then start the campaign there.

Say plainly that the story continues from the summary, not from a live transfer: the full game has no access to this chat. Offer a checkpoint export too if the player wants the portable state alongside the summary.
