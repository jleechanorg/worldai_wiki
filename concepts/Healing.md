# Healing

How you get HP back, and when it is worth spending a turn on.

Rests are the biggest source of healing and they have their own page —
see [RestAndDeath](RestAndDeath.md) for short rests, hit dice, and long rests.
This page covers everything else.

## Sources of healing

- **Healing spells**: Cure Wounds restores 1d8 + your spellcasting modifier and
  takes your action; Healing Word restores 1d4 + your spellcasting modifier and
  takes only a bonus action; Prayer of Healing takes ten minutes of casting and
  restores 2d8 + your spellcasting modifier to as many as six people you can
  see within 30 feet, so it is a between-fights spell.
- **Class features**: a Paladin's Lay on Hands is a pool of HP equal to five
  times their level, spent an action at a time and refilled on a long rest.
- **Potions of Healing**: 2d4+2 HP (common), 4d4+4 (greater), 8d4+8 (superior),
  10d4+20 (supreme). Drinking one costs your action in combat.
- **Rests**: hit dice on a short rest, full HP on a long rest
  ([RestAndDeath](RestAndDeath.md)).

Upcasting works on healing spells: Cure Wounds in a 3rd-level slot heals
3d8 + your modifier instead of 1d8 ([Spellcasting](Spellcasting.md)).

## When healing matters most

- **Mid-combat**: healing is worth an action mainly to pull someone off death
  saves or keep a front-liner standing. Otherwise, killing the enemy faster
  usually ends the damage sooner than healing outpaces it.
- **Between fights**: this is where short rests and hit dice do the work.
- **After a hard day**: a long rest puts you back to full.

## Player tips

- **Healing Word beats Cure Wounds in a fight.** It costs a bonus action, so
  you can pick an ally up *and* still attack on the same turn.
- **Any healing at all ends the dying state.** A single HP wakes someone who is
  making death saves, so a small heal at the right moment is worth more than a
  big one later.
- **Don't enter a dungeon at half HP.** A long rest first is nearly always
  cheaper than the fight you lose without one.
