# Campaign Showcase

Each campaign below is a real run by a real player, written up as a case study — what one player did, not what you have to do. Borrow the patterns you like and ignore the rest.

If your idea isn't represented here, that says nothing about what the game can run. It only means nobody has written that campaign up yet.

## Published campaigns

### Itachi Gaiden — long escalation across hundreds of scenes

A 432-scene Naruto campaign that grows from "ANBU rookie" to multiverse-spanning transcendence, on a two-line opening premise and a single one-sentence tone directive. The best example in the wiki of a campaign that escalates for hundreds of scenes without losing its voice.

Full breakdown: [ItachiGaiden](ItachiGaiden.md).

### Aristocrat Reborn — character study and internal monologue

A 50-scene isekai campaign about Sylphina, the seventh daughter of a Margrave, reincarnated from a magic-obsessed past life. Built on a ~3,100-word opening premise that spells out her psychology, and narrated largely through her own thoughts. Read this one for prompt length, the "obsessive specialist" archetype, and the gap between narrative power and character-sheet power.

Full breakdown: [AristocratReborn](AristocratReborn.md).

### Nocturne BG3 V3 — running a faction, not just a character

A 600+ scene Baldur's Gate 3 campaign played with faction mode on: intel gathering, faction combat, power rankings, resources, and diplomacy across Goblins, Tieflings, Druids and Duergar. The reference run for faction play.

Full breakdown: [NocturneBg3](NocturneBg3.md).

## Runs with no case study

These campaigns were played. Nobody has written them up:

- **Frieren-style campaign** — slow-burn fantasy: quiet scenes, atmospheric prose, time-skips across decades, a small premise stretched into a long life.
- **Luke-style campaign** — a Star Wars-flavoured hero's journey: farm boy, call to adventure, trials, legend.
- **Sariel** — an angel as the player character, in a high-celestial setting; a look at how a non-human origin changes what you can do and how NPCs react.
- **Sariel, second campaign** — the same character carried into a different world: same name, same personality, new setting.

## Designing a campaign that isn't any of these

The campaigns above are illustrations, not templates. To build your own:

1. **Start with the setting**, not the character — see [CampaignDesign](../concepts/CampaignDesign.md).
2. **Pick a tone** that fits it, and write it down.
3. **Write an opening premise** specific enough that a stranger could narrate scene one from it.
4. **Add one to three directives** that pin the voice — see [GodModePrompting](../concepts/GodModePrompting.md).
5. **Plan the first ten scenes**, not the whole arc. The arc comes out of play.

For a ready-made campaign you can paste and start in three minutes, see the [House of the Dragon template](../queries/house-of-the-dragon-campaign.md). For your first session generally, see [how-to-play-worldai](../queries/how-to-play-worldai.md).
