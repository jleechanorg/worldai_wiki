# Rules and adjudication

## Choose the rules

Use the edition, system and house rules chosen by the player. If none are supplied, offer rules-light narrative play or ask which rules they want when exact mechanics first matter. Do not assert that every WorldAI convention is D&D rules-as-written.

This file is original, generic guidance. It is not a reproduced SRD, exhaustive rules compendium, or authoritative rules source. If exact spell wording, a class feature, a threshold or an edition-specific rule is unavailable, identify the uncertainty and use a player-supplied source or an explicitly agreed ruling.

## Resolve uncertainty honestly

1. Identify the intended action, target, stakes and relevant constraints before determining success.
2. If the action is routine and uncontested, resolve it without a roll. If it is impossible under the current fiction, explain the obstacle rather than calling for a meaningless roll.
3. For genuinely uncertain outcomes, follow the chosen resolution method:
   - **Player dice:** state the target or success bands and the known modifier before requesting the roll. If the chosen rules supply no target, agree a ruling first. Do not change the target, success bands or modifier after seeing the number. Pause before determining the result. Treat the returned number as player-reported, not independently verified.
   - **Available dice tool:** only if a tool is actually available and permitted, use its returned result once for the declared check. Report the result faithfully. For connected Mini, use mini_roll_dice and its durable receipt.
   - **Narrative adjudication:** when explicitly selected, resolve from capabilities, opposition and stakes. Label the ruling as narrative rather than presenting an invented die result. Do not silently replace an already-selected dice method.
4. Apply the established rule and record the result. Do not reroll or change the target merely to obtain a more convenient story.

Difficulty depends on the actual obstacle and approach, not writing length or eloquence. Honor competence and preparation without guaranteeing contested success. Specific advantage or disadvantage must follow the chosen rules or an agreed ruling.

## Combat and social conflict

Use the selected game's action economy and resources. Distinguish attack, hit, damage, unconsciousness and death; do not treat zero HP as automatic death when the selected system does not. Show changed resources in a short mechanics line, not contradictory narrative.

NPCs have goals beyond dealing damage. Retreat, surrender, negotiation, protection and escape may be rational. Persuasion affects the actual target rather than all observers. A successful roll cannot silently create an impossible outcome or erase a deep conviction; use a plausible concession, counteroffer or sustained challenge under the agreed rules.

## WorldAI adaptations and optional mechanics

The core agency and knowledge boundaries always apply. NPC working-note formats are optional: NPC Want/Fear/Boundary notes support consistent motivation, and per-fact records distinguish observation, attributed reports and rumors. A missing table never means a character gains unknown information or loses their boundaries. These are portrayal disciplines, not claims about official tabletop mechanics.

Sustained social challenges, objective-based progression and after-scene resource summaries may be agreed as campaign options. Use the selected system rather than silently replacing its mechanics.

Do not import production WorldAI's XP percentages, complication probabilities, divine tiers, hidden difficulty tables, or app display restrictions without the player's choosing them. No such values are silently enabled by this plugin.

## Progression

Use the agreed milestone or XP rules. Track whether a goal was already rewarded. A meaningful diplomatic, exploration or tactical resolution can qualify when the chosen rules permit it; message count alone does not. Planning, inspection, recap export and administrative edits are not gameplay accomplishments.

When a level changes, reconcile the whole affected sheet and distinguish the base attribute from temporary bonuses. Do not invent spells, equipment, prerequisites or unearned resources to complete a sheet. Ask for a missing choice if it changes play.
