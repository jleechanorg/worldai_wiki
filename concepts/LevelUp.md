# Level Up

When your XP total crosses a threshold, your character levels up on that same
turn. Nothing interrupts the scene — the header shows the new level, a level-up
banner appears in the story log, and your ordinary story choices are still there
to click.

The AI applies a complete, rules-legal package for you: HP, class and subclass
features, an ability score increase or feat when one is due, and new spells. You
can change any of it afterwards, or ignore it and keep playing.

## What a level gives you

- **HP increase** — your class hit die plus your Constitution modifier
- **Class features** at the levels your class grants them
- **Subclass features** — see [Subclass](Subclass.md)
- **Ability score increase or feat** at 4th, 8th, 12th, 16th and 19th for most
  classes — see [ASI](ASI.md)
- **Spell slots and spells** if you cast
- **Fighting style** if your class grants one (Fighters at 1st level; Rangers
  and Paladins at 2nd)

|-------|------------------------------|
| 1 | 0 |
| 2 | 300 |
| 3 | 900 |
| 4 | 2,700 |
| 5 | 6,500 |
| 6 | 14,000 |
| 7 | 23,000 |
| 8 | 34,000 |
| 9 | 48,000 |
| 10 | 64,000 |
| 11 | 85,000 |
| 12 | 100,000 |
| 13 | 120,000 |
| 14 | 140,000 |
| 15 | 165,000 |
| 16 | 195,000 |
| 17 | 225,000 |
| 18 | 265,000 |
| 19 | 305,000 |
| 20 | 355,000 |

These are running totals, not per-level costs — you hit level 5 when your XP
total reaches 6,500, not when you earn another 6,500.

XP is awarded sparingly. Routine turns give nothing; a level typically takes many
sessions. See [LevelUpProgression](LevelUpProgression.md) for the pace and for
what happens past level 20.

## Changing what the AI picked

A **Review Level-Up Choices** option sits among your story choices from the
moment you level until you use it. Opening it shows the whole package that was
already applied — HP, subclass, ability increase or feat, spells — pre-filled and
editable together, not one slot at a time.

Change whatever you like. Each change is saved immediately and the review stays
open, so you can keep adjusting. A single finish option closes the review and
hands you back to the story. Your level and XP do not change again when you
finish.

You can also leave the review unopened indefinitely. The AI's package is already
in effect either way.

## Player tips

- **Know your ASI levels**: 4th, 8th, 12th, 16th, 19th. If you want a specific
  feat, decide before you get there and name it in the review.
- **Read your new features**: the banner names what you gained. Knowing you now
  have a second attack or a new spell changes what you should try next.
- **The review is not a deadline.** It waits. Finish the scene first if you'd
  rather not break the mood.

See [ASI](ASI.md), [Subclass](Subclass.md), and
