# Campaign Wizard

The guided UI flow for creating a new campaign. See
[CampaignWizard](../concepts/CampaignWizard.md) for the player-facing
guide.

## The 2 steps

1. **Choose Type** — pick the built-in Dragon Knight preset or a Custom
   campaign; pre-fills title, character, and setting defaults based on
   the pick.
2. **Ready to Launch** — review and edit the Campaign Summary, optionally
   upload an avatar, click **Enter the World**.

Each step takes 30 seconds to a few minutes depending on how much you
customize.

## What happens after

The campaign starts in Scene 1. The GM narrates the opening based on
your campaign-type + character + setting choices, and prompts you for
your first action.
